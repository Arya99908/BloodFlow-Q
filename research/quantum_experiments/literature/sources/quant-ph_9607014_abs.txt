      Title: A Quantum Algorithm for Finding the Minimum 
      Authors:  Christoph Durr ,  Peter Hoyer               View a PDF of the paper titled A Quantum Algorithm for Finding the Minimum, by Christoph Durr and Peter Hoyer 
     View PDF 
     HTML (experimental) 



     
             Abstract:   We give a quantum algorithm to find the index y in a table T of size N such that in time O(c sqrt N), T[y] is minimum with probability at least 1-1/2^c.
